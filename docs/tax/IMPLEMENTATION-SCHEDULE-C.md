# Implementation: Schedule C + SE tax (vertical slice)

**Implements:** [GAP-SCHEDULE-C.md](./GAP-SCHEDULE-C.md) / gap PR [#3](https://github.com/jz4396/direct-file/pull/3)
**Tax year:** TY2026, filed in 2027
**Blast radius:** **high** — total income / AGI, ACTC earned income, Schedule 1 line 15 (§ 164(f)) adjustment, Schedule 2 line 21 / Form 1040 line 23–24 (SE tax).

## What this slice ships

1. Interview gate for self-employment / gig / freelance income (`/hadSelfEmploymentIncome`)
2. One or more Schedule C businesses (name, gross receipts, MVP expense total)
3. Net profit/(loss) `/scheduleCNetProfitOrLoss` → Schedule 1 line 3 → `income.xml` `/otherIncome` (Schedule 1 line 10 → Form 1040 line 8) → `/totalIncome` (Form 1040 line 9)
4. Schedule SE, one per filer with SE income (#118): net earnings = that filer's net profit × 92.35%, 12.4% OASDI (wage base reduced by that filer's own W-2 boxes 3 and 7, § 1402(b)(1)), 2.9% HI. Schedule 2 line 4 = sum of each line 12; Schedule 1 line 15 = sum of each line 13; Form 8959 line 13 = sum of each line 6
5. `/selfEmploymentTax` → Schedule 2 line 4 / line 21 (`/line21OfSchedule2`) → Form 1040 line 23 → `/totalTax` (line 24)
6. `/deductibleSelfEmploymentTax` (one-half, § 164(f)) → Schedule 1 line 15 → `/adjustmentsToIncome` → `/agi`
7. ACTC earned income (`/earnedIncomeWithAllCombatPay`, Schedule 8812 line 18a) += net profit − deductible SE tax
8. **Explicit knockouts (no silent zero / no silent mis-statement)**, both `BlockSubmissionOnTrue` and in `/flowIsKnockedOut`:
   - `/flowKnockoutScheduleCQbi` — § 199A QBI deduction requested
   - `/flowKnockoutScheduleCSpouseBusiness` — any business owned by the secondary filer. Schedule SE is figured per spouse, but EITC/CDCC earned income with self-employment are not per spouse yet, and no Schedule SE PDF is printed yet
   - `/flowKnockoutScheduleCEarnedIncomeCredits` — a Schedule C loss when the EITC could apply (AGI below the completed phaseout) or the CDCC or CTC applies, since `/scheduleCNetProfitForEarnedIncome` floors a loss at zero; or a joint return with a Schedule C claiming the CDCC (Form 2441 lines 4 and 5 need per-spouse Schedule SE)
   - `/flowKnockoutScheduleSEOutOfScope` — Schedule SE lines this tool does not compute, asked only after self-employment income is yes or a nonzero Form 1065 box 14 code A: farm profit, Conservation Reserve Program payments, and the farm optional method (lines 1a, 1b, and Part II line 15; 26 U.S.C. 1402(a), 1402(a)(1), 1402(l)), ministry or a Form 4361 or Form 4029 exemption (1402(a)(8), 1402(c)(4), 1402(e), 1402(g)), Form 8919 wages (line 8c), and the secondary filer's church employee income (26 U.S.C. 3121(w), 1402(b), 1402(j)). Notary net profit (1402(c)(1); 2026 draft Instructions for Schedule SE, PDF p. 5, Line 2 item 2) is subtracted on line 3 when other net earnings are $400 or more. A notary-only return, a notary amount on a loss, and a notary amount above Schedule C line 31 still stop. The primary filer's church employee income is computed on lines 5a and 5b (3121(w), 1402(a)(12), 1402(a)(14), 1402(j)): line 5b is line 5a times 92.35 percent, and is zero when that result is under $100. Line 4c under $400 is zero on line 6, and line 6 adds line 5b. The spouse's amount is not entered on line 5a. On a joint return that question must be answered; a yes stops the return. A return that is not married filing jointly does not ask it. An unanswered question stays incomplete. Every stopping answer no leaves those lines blank and keeps the regular Schedule SE computation.
   - `/flowKnockoutScheduleSENonfarmOptionalMethod` — the nonfarm optional method (line 4b and Part II lines 16 and 17; 26 U.S.C. 1402(a), 1402(l)). Line 16 is $7,560 (1402(l)(1), four 2026 quarters of coverage at $1,890). Line 17 is two-thirds of Schedule C line 7, not more than line 16, and not less than actual net earnings. Schedule C line 31 must be less than $8,186 and less than 72.189% of Schedule C line 7. The filer must have had net earnings of at least $400 in 2 of the prior 3 years and fewer than five prior uses. Schedule K-1 (Form 1065) box 14 code A together with the election stops. This fact is part of `/scheduleCSectionIsComplete`. When the method applies, lines 2, 3, and 4a are $0 and line 4c equals line 4b.
   - `/flowKnockoutScheduleSENonfarmOptionalMethodCredits` — the method applies and the return claims the earned income credit, the additional child tax credit, or the child and dependent care credit. Line 4b is not added to earned income. 2026 draft Instructions for Form 2441, PDF p. 5, lines 4 and 5: subtract Schedule 1 line 15 from Schedule SE lines 3 and 4b. Not part of `/scheduleCSectionIsComplete`.
   - Additional Medicare tax on self-employment income is computed on Form 8959 (26 U.S.C. 1401(b)(2)). `/flowKnockoutScheduleCAdditionalMedicare` is not used.
9. Section is required: `/scheduleCSectionIsComplete` is part of `flow.xml` `/flowIncomplete`
10. Unset `/scheduleCBusinesses` collection: "no" (or section not reached) → $0, complete; "yes" with no businesses → incomplete, so `/totalTaxIsIncomplete` blocks submission

## Line-item expenses

A return that only stored one expense total still uses that total, and lines 2, 4, and 6 are zero. Once any Part I amount, stop answer, or expense answer is entered, the single total is ignored and Schedule C is figured from the lines.

Supported on that path: lines 1 through 8, 10, 11, 14, 15, 16a, 16c, 17 through 19, 20a, 20b, and 21 through 26. Line 24b is half of the meals entered, rounded half up (26 U.S.C. 274(n)). Part V accepts three other expenses; their total is lines 48 and 27b. Line 28 is the sum of those lines and does not include business use of the home. Line 29 is line 7 minus line 28. Line 30, when the simplified method applies, is $5 times the square feet used for business, not more than 300, and not more than line 29 (if line 29 is zero or a loss, line 30 is zero). Line 31 is line 29 minus line 30. Two or more businesses still add into Schedule 1 line 3 and, for one filer, into Schedule SE line 2 before the 92.35 percent factor. A loss with material participation and every investment at risk (line 32a) flows, unless Schedule 1 line 3 is a loss of more than $128,000. Anything else below stops the return, and an unanswered required amount stays incomplete rather than zero.

Simple Part III is figured. 2026 Schedule C, PDF p. 2, line 4: "Cost of goods sold (from line 42)." Line 35 is inventory at the beginning of the year. Line 36 is "Purchases less cost of items withdrawn for personal use" (purchases and withdrawals are entered separately; the form has no separate personal-use line). Line 37 is cost of labor, not including amounts paid to yourself. Line 38 is materials and supplies. Line 39 is other costs. Line 40: "Add lines 35 through 39." Line 41 is inventory at the end of the year. Line 42: "Cost of goods sold. Subtract line 41 from line 40. Enter the result here and on line 4." Each of those derived lines is rounded half up to the nearest dollar, the same way the other Schedule C lines in this tool are rounded. The result is Schedule C line 4, then line 5, line 7, line 31, Schedule 1 line 3, and Schedule SE line 2.

That path applies only when the business has inventory, line 33 is cost (33a) or lower of cost or market (33b), line 34 is no, line 42 is at least zero, line 35 equals last year's closing inventory, the filer is not a tax shelter, and the average annual gross receipts for the 3 prior tax years do not exceed $32,000,000. Rev. Proc. 2025-32 §4.30, PDF p. 23: the § 448(c) test "does not exceed $32,000,000." The instructions, PDF p. 16, say a small business taxpayer has "average annual gross receipts of $32 million or less for the 3 prior tax years (indexed for inflation), and (b) are not a tax shelter (as defined in section 448(d)(3))." 26 U.S.C. 471(c)(1) and 263A(i)(1) use that test. The filer enters the 3-prior-year average. This year's line 1 is not the test. An average equal to $32,000,000 still uses simple Part III. An average greater than $32,000,000 stops it. A tax shelter stops it. Line 42 below zero is not replaced with zero; it stops the return. Line 35 different from last year's closing inventory stops the return because the form says to attach an explanation. Section 263A uniform capitalization is not computed, and neither is a LIFO or other method.

An answer of no to inventory, with Part III left blank or zero, keeps line 4 at zero. The section 471(c)(1)(B)(i) method (inventory treated as non-incidental materials and supplies) with every Part III amount blank or zero is the same zero. Saying there is no inventory while a cost, lower-of-cost-or-market, or other method is selected, or while any Part III amount is greater than zero, stops the return. Saying there is inventory and then choosing that no-inventory method also stops it. Line 33c ("Other (attach explanation)") stops. Line 34 yes ("Was there any change in determining quantities, costs, or valuations between opening and closing inventory?") stops. An unanswered method, line 34, or amount on the simple path leaves line 4 incomplete. A stopped return does not publish line 4 as zero.

`PrintRefusingKnockouts.FACT_PATHS` did not gain or lose a path. `/flowKnockoutScheduleCOutOfScope` is still the print-refuse fact and is an operand of `/flowIsKnockedOut`. It is no longer true merely because the business has inventory valued at cost or lower of cost or market, with line 34 no, line 42 at least zero, line 35 equal to last year's closing inventory, a 3-prior-year average that does not exceed $32,000,000, and the filer not a tax shelter. It is still true for line 33c, a line 34 change, a 3-prior-year average greater than $32,000,000, a tax shelter, line 42 below zero, line 35 different from last year's closing inventory, the section 471(c) no-inventory method while Part III is required, Part III filled in after a no to inventory, and the other stops below. An unanswered Part III question stays incomplete, and an incomplete knockout refuses the print. Line 35's difference fact, and the line 42 sign fact, are complete and false when the business has no inventory path.

Line 30 uses the simplified method only in the worksheet case this tool can figure. 2026 draft Instructions for Schedule C, PDF p. 14, Simplified Method Worksheet: "3a. Maximum allowable amount ... $5." Line 2: "Allowable square footage ... Don't enter more than 300 square feet." Line 5: "Allowable expenses using the simplified method. Enter the smaller of line 1 or line 4 here and include that amount on Schedule C, line 30. If zero or less, enter -0-." PDF p. 13, Line 1: if all gross income is from the qualified business use of the home, the limit starts with Schedule C line 29. PDF p. 12: "You are limited to a maximum of 300 square feet for all of the businesses you conduct in your home." The area on line 30(b) is the area actually used. The deduction uses the smaller of that area and 300. The $5 amount and the 300 square foot maximum are tax year 2026 facts. Rev. Proc. 2013-13. 26 U.S.C. 280A(c)(5). A business area larger than the home leaves line 30 incomplete and is not a stop. Shared space stops the return: PDF p. 12, "You must allocate the shared space between you and the other person in a reasonable manner." Rental use stops the return: PDF p. 11, "You can't use the simplified method to figure a deduction for rental use of your home." A prior Form 8829 carryover stops the return. PDF p. 12: those actual expenses "can't" be claimed while using the simplified method, and worksheet lines 6a and 6b (PDF p. 14) are not tracked. Yes on each of those questions is the case this tool can figure. No stops the return. An unanswered question stays incomplete.

Stopped, and not printed as zero: an accounting method other than cash or accrual (line F; Form 3115 is not filed), Part III when it cannot be figured (line 33c, a line 34 change, a 3-prior-year average greater than $32,000,000, a tax shelter, line 42 below zero, line 35 different from last year's closing inventory, the section 471(c) no-inventory method while Part III is required, or Part III filled in after a no to inventory), car and truck expenses and vehicle-loan interest (lines 9 and 16b, Form 4562), depreciation and section 179 (line 13, Form 4562), depletion (line 12), rent or lease of a vehicle (line 20a; the inclusion amount in Publication 463, chapter 4, and listed property under 26 U.S.C. 280F(c) and 280F(d)(4)), Form 8829 (actual expenses for business use of the home), shared space, rental use of the home, a prior Form 8829 carryover, a simplified method that is not the line 29 worksheet (a second home, part-year use, an area that changed, a daycare fraction other than 1.0, gross income that is not all from the home, or a Form 8949 or Form 4797 adjustment), more than one business electing the simplified method, the energy efficient commercial buildings deduction (line 27a, Form 7205), meals that are not limited to 50 percent, wages reduced by Form 5884, 8932, or 8994, mortgage or other interest that must be allocated, amortization that begins this year (Form 4562-B), a fourth Part V line, line 6 income that is a fuel credit or Form 4797 recapture, a loss without material participation (Form 8582, 26 U.S.C. 469), a loss that is not fully at risk (line 32b, Form 6198, 26 U.S.C. 465), and a Schedule 1 line 3 loss of more than $128,000 (Form 461, 26 U.S.C. 461(l)). Pennsylvania does not follow the federal safe harbor. PA-40 C (EX) 03-26 instructions, PDF p. 4: "Pennsylvania does not recognize the federal safe harbor method." A line-item business that claims a home office stops the Pennsylvania return. PA Schedule C-2 is not computed.

## Form 461 excess business loss

`/flowKnockoutScheduleCOutOfScope` is still the only Schedule C print-refuse path. It is also true when `/scheduleCNeedsForm461` is true: the filer has Schedule C businesses and `/scheduleCNetProfitOrLoss` (Schedule 1 (Form 1040), line 3, which is Form 461, line 2) is a loss of more than `/form461SingleLineLossLimit` ($128,000). A loss of exactly $128,000 still flows. The comparison is zero minus that net, so one business, a single expense total, and the sum of several businesses use the same Schedule 1 line 3 amount. The per-business out-of-scope collection is checked first, so a form stop stays complete true before expense amounts are entered. While that net is still incomplete and no business is already out of scope, the Form 461 check is false, so an unstarted expense total is not a stop. An unanswered line-item question on a business that was started still leaves the knockout incomplete.

`/form461SingleLineLossLimit` is $128,000. 2026 draft Instructions for Form 461, PDF p. 2, Who Must File (ii): "you would report a loss of more than $128,000 on any one of Form 461, lines 1 through 8." On a return that is not already stopped for every Schedule E loss, Schedule F and Form 4797 are unsupported, and a capital loss is capped and excluded under 26 U.S.C. 461(l)(3)(B)(i), so Schedule 1 line 3 is the only Form 461 line 1 through 8 that can be a loss.

`/excessBusinessLossThreshold` is $512,000 when `/isFilingStatusMFJ` and $256,000 otherwise, including single, head of household, married filing separately, and qualifying surviving spouse. 26 U.S.C. 461(l)(3)(A)(ii)(II) applies 200 percent only "in the case of a joint return." Rev. Proc. 2025-32 §4.31, PDF p. 23: "For taxable years beginning in 2026, in determining a taxpayer's excess business loss, the amount under § 461(l)(3)(A)(ii)(II) is $256,000 ($512,000 for joint returns)." 2026 draft Form 461, PDF p. 2, line 15: "Enter $256,000 (or $512,000 if married filing jointly)." Who Must File (i) disallows a loss only when it is more than $256,000 ($512,000 on a joint return). Stopping at more than $128,000 on Schedule 1 line 3 also stops every return that would have a disallowed excess business loss.

Form 461 is not attached, and the disallowed amount is not added back on Schedule 1, line 8p. 2026 draft Schedule 1, PDF p. 2, line 8p: "Section 461(l) excess business loss adjustment." 26 U.S.C. 461(l)(1)(B): "any excess business loss of the taxpayer for the taxable year shall not be allowed." 26 U.S.C. 461(l)(2): "Any loss which is disallowed under paragraph (1) shall be treated as a net operating loss for the taxable year for purposes of determining any net operating loss carryover under section 172(b) for subsequent taxable years." 2026 draft Instructions for Schedule C, PDF p. 3: "Form 461 to report an excess business loss." Filing and public print stop instead of reporting the uncapped loss in AGI or on Form 6251.

`/scheduleCNeedsForm461` is complete false when self-employment income was not asked, was answered no, or there is no Schedule C business.

## Header lines H, I, and J

Interview and print only. Answering them does not change net profit, Schedule 1 line 3, Schedule SE, or AGI. Each question is required and stays incomplete until answered. There is no default of no.

Line H is one checkbox on each business. 2026 draft Instructions for Schedule C, PDF p. 6, Line H: "If you started or acquired this business in 2026, check the box on line H. Also, check the box if you are reopening or restarting this business after temporarily closing it, and you didn’t file a 2025 Schedule C for this business." Yes checks the box. No leaves it unchecked.

Line I is Yes or No. Same PDF p. 6, Line I: "If you made any payment in 2026 that would require you to file any Forms 1099, check the “Yes” box. Otherwise, check the “No” box." "For 2026, the information reporting threshold for certain payments reported on Forms 1099 increased from $600 to $2,000." 26 U.S.C. 6041(a), as amended by P.L. 119-21. Neither box is checked until the filer answers.

Line J is Yes or No, and it is asked only when line I is Yes. 2026 Schedule C, PDF p. 1, line J: "If “Yes,” did you or will you file required Form(s) 1099?" When line I is No, line J is complete false and is not printed. PA-40 C has no matching lines for federal H, I, or J. `PrintRefusingKnockouts` is unchanged.

## Line 20a

The question is split. Rent or lease of a vehicle still stops the return. 2026 draft Instructions for Schedule C, PDF p. 9, Line 20a: "But if you leased a vehicle for a term of 30 days or more, you may have to reduce your deduction by the inclusion amount. See Leasing a Car in chapter 4 of Pub. 463." A vehicle is listed property under 26 U.S.C. 280F(d)(4), and 26 U.S.C. 280F(c) limits the deduction. This tool does not compute the inclusion amount, and it does not file Form 4562 for that lease. The vehicle portion of line 20a stays blank.

Rent of machinery or equipment that is not a vehicle is the business-use portion of the rental cost, deductible under 26 U.S.C. 162(a)(3). The same instructions, PDF p. 9: "If you rented or leased vehicles, machinery, or equipment, enter on line 20a the business-use portion of your rental cost." That amount prints on line 20a and is included in line 28. Line 31, Schedule 1 line 3, and Schedule SE line 2 follow from that. Line 20b is unchanged. Leasing property that is not listed property does not require Form 4562. An answer of no, or an entered zero, is zero. An unanswered question or a blank amount stays incomplete. PA-40 C line 25, Rent on business property, is federal line 20a plus federal line 20b, the same place line 20b already prints. `PrintRefusingKnockouts` did not gain or lose a path. `/flowKnockoutScheduleCOutOfScope` still refuses a vehicle lease. It does not refuse machinery and equipment rent that is not a vehicle.

## Out of scope (explicit)

- The line 1 statutory-employee checkbox. Lines A (activity, including product or service), B (a six-digit code from the 2026 draft chart), D (EIN, blank if none), E (business address, blank on the federal form when the business is at the address on the return), F (cash or accrual), H, I, and J print. Line F other stops the return and is not printed. Line C, the business name, already printed. The same facts fill PA-40 C main business activity, product or service, EIN, business address, Federal NAICS Code, and the cash or accrual oval. An unanswered required fact stays blank and blocks the section. PA-40 C has no matching lines for federal H, I, or J.
- Part V lines 4 through 9 (a fourth other expense stops the return)
- Form 461 itself, and Schedule 1 line 8p. A Schedule 1 line 3 loss of more than $128,000 stops the return (`/scheduleCNeedsForm461`) instead of attaching Form 461 or adding back a disallowed excess business loss
- Statutory employees (already stopped on the Form W-2). Notary net profit is subtracted on Schedule SE line 3 when other net earnings are $400 or more (26 U.S.C. 1402(c)(1); 2026 draft Instructions for Schedule SE, PDF p. 5, Line 2 item 2). A notary-only return, a notary amount on a loss, and a notary amount above line 31 still stop on `/flowKnockoutScheduleSEOutOfScope`. The farm optional method and farm income stop there instead of being left blank. The primary filer's church employee income is Schedule SE lines 5a and 5b (26 U.S.C. 3121(w), 1402(j)). The spouse's church employee income stops the return and is not entered on line 5a. The nonfarm optional method is computed when the filer elects it and the tests are met
- Additional 0.9% Medicare tax is computed on Form 8959 (26 U.S.C. 1401(b)(2))
- QBI § 199A computation (knocked out)
- EITC / CDCC earned income from a Schedule C loss, and CDCC on a joint return with a Schedule C (knocked out, see above). A profit is included per filer: that filer's Schedule SE line 3 (Schedule C line 31 minus notary net profit; 26 U.S.C. 1402(c)(1) and 32(c)(2)(A)(ii)) minus that filer's Schedule SE line 13 (Form 1040 instructions (2025) EIC Worksheet B, PDF p. 47; 2026 draft Form 2441 instructions, PDF p. 5)
- MFJ spouse-owned business (per-spouse Schedule SE facts exist; the knockout stays until EITC/CDCC earned income is per spouse and a Schedule SE PDF prints for each spouse)
- Public print of a Schedule C return is still refused because Form 8995 has no 2026 template. The 2026 Schedule C final is mapped. No MeF for these lines yet. The Spanish Schedule C draft is not in the package.
